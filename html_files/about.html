MEA works with heavy equipment and engineering solutions for the industrial sector, led by Eng. M. Said Mughrabi, who has spent his working life on industrial projects since 1990.

What we provide

We are based in Germany and work with clients worldwide.

For many years our own factory built and overhauled the machines you see on these pages, to high standards and with skilled workers led by Eng. M. Said Mughrabi.

Eng. M. Said Mughrabi provides design, consulting and site supervision across the disciplines the work depends on: mechanical, electrical, electronics and IT.

We care about our clients' ambitions and their growth, so we support them in facing the challenges ahead — putting our experience at their disposal to be sure they reach the position they want in the market.

Capabilities

Three things we are built to do

01 — PRODUCTS

Our products mainly cover the electrical and mechanical sectors: systems, machines, plants and production lines — with the services that suit our clients, including manufacturing, development, repair, maintenance and PLC programming. We are distinguished by giving clients full designs matched to their required production capacity.

Our track record includes

  • Concrete batching plants — fixed and portable, highly customisable. We are proud to be one of the innovative companies manufacturing these in all types.
  • Brick and block making machines
  • Conveyors
  • Metal treatment machines
  • Crushers, grinding mills, ore beneficiation, washing and screening machines
  • All types of production lines, with PLC programming
02 — ENGINEERING SOLUTIONS

Manufacturers face many obstacles during operation: breakdowns, machinery updates, and modifications needed to reach an estimated production capacity. Usually they procure whole new production lines to meet growth, spending large amounts of capital.

MEA offers all of those services and more. You can design, manufacture and program your production line locally, and benefit from far better after-sales service at a better price. We carry your requirement through to the suitable solution and design, implemented as a system that solves the task.

We also take care of

  • Metal segregation
  • Waste management
  • Production lines needing crushing and screening
  • Any mechanical or electrical system you require
03 — PROCUREMENT

Our purchasing department is experienced in global sourcing and the import of a wide variety of equipment, based on each client's exact requirement.
