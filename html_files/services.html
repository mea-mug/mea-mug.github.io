Services

Everything to do with mechanical design and production.

Five service lines, from a first feasibility study through to a running production line and the service that keeps it running.

The five service lines

01

Industrial engineering works

Design and manufacture of machines and systems built for a specific job — including special-purpose machines you cannot buy off the shelf.

02

Industrial equipment supply

Machine tools and industrial equipment sourced, imported and installed — lathes, milling, drilling, shaping, presses and the full CNC range.

03

Machines & production line supply

Complete turnkey lines: specified, supplied, installed, commissioned and PLC-programmed, then supported afterwards.

04

Industrial engineering consultancy

Drawings, schemes, specifications and site supervision — including stalled projects that need an outside eye to get moving again.

05

Feasibility studies

Before the money is spent: what the plant will cost, what it will produce, what it will earn, and whether the numbers actually work. We have produced these for food, metal and chemical plants alike.

After the handover

A machine you can keep running

Buying a line abroad is easy. Keeping it running once the container has left is where most factories get stuck.

Because we design and build locally, spare parts, modifications and repairs stay local too — and so does the engineer who knows the machine.

  • Repair and maintenance
  • Machine development and upgrades
  • PLC programming and re-programming
  • Full after-sales service
